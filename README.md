# AI Supply Chain ERP Automation

### AI-Enabled Supply Chain & ERP Process Automation Framework

A professional portfolio project by **Faysal Ahmed (FaysalAhmedAIERP)** focused on combining Supply Chain Management, ERP workflows, Python automation, SOP design and AI-assisted decision support.

---

## Project Overview

This project explores how Artificial Intelligence can support structured Supply Chain and ERP processes while maintaining human accountability and controlled ERP execution.

The framework is designed around the principle:

> **AI recommends and explains; ERP executes approved actions; Humans remain accountable.**

The objective is to transform repetitive Supply Chain and ERP activities into structured, traceable and automation-ready workflows.

## Proposed Architecture

### 1. Business Process Layer
Captures operational Supply Chain and procurement activities.

Examples:

- Purchase requisition
- Vendor sourcing
- Quotation comparison
- Purchase order processing
- Shipment follow-up
- Warehouse receiving
- Inventory monitoring
- Commercial documentation

### 2. SOP & Rules Layer

Business procedures are converted into structured rules and workflow steps.

Examples:

- Approval requirements
- Document validation
- Safety-stock rules
- Vendor-selection criteria
- Reorder conditions
- Procurement controls

### 3. AI Assistance Layer

AI supports users through:

- Recommendations
- Document analysis
- Workflow guidance
- Exception identification
- Process explanations
- Decision-support information

### 4. Human Approval Layer

Critical business decisions remain subject to authorized human review and approval.

### 5. ERP Execution Layer

Approved actions can then be processed through ERP or enterprise systems.
